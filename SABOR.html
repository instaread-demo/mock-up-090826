21st Century ROAD to Housing Act Signed Into Law

By erika.toney@sabor.com / July 15, 2026
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 2:30
Instaread

On July 10, the 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act was signed into law, marking a significant advocacy victory for REALTORS® and the housing industry after nearly two years of collaboration led by the National Association of REALTORS® (NAR).

While no single piece of legislation can solve today’s housing affordability challenges overnight, this law represents an important step toward increasing housing opportunities and addressing one of the nation’s biggest long-term issues: housing supply.

Why This Matters

Across the country, and here in the San Antonio region, housing affordability continues to be a concern for buyers, sellers and communities alike. The cost of homeownership is influenced by many factors, including mortgage rates, construction costs, available inventory and local market conditions.

The 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act won’t immediately lower home prices. Instead, it supports policies designed to help expand the nation’s housing supply over time, creating more opportunities for future homeowners and strengthening local housing markets.

What It Means for San Antonio

Housing affordability continues to be a concern for buyers, sellers and communities across the San Antonio region. While home prices are influenced by many factors, including mortgage rates, construction costs and local market conditions, one of the biggest long-term challenges remains housing supply.

SABOR’s June 2026 Market Statistics show the San Antonio area has 6.13 months of housing inventory, a level generally considered to represent a more balanced market. While buyers today have more choices than they did just a few years ago, increasing housing opportunities remains essential to keeping pace with our region’s continued growth and supporting long-term affordability.

The 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act won’t immediately lower home prices. Instead, it includes provisions intended to support long-term efforts to expand housing opportunities and address the nation’s housing supply challenges. More housing options over time can create a healthier, more resilient market for buyers, sellers and the communities we serve.

REALTOR® Advocacy in Action

Addressing housing affordability requires action at every level. Federal legislation is one piece of the puzzle, alongside state policies, local planning and private-sector investment.

SABOR is committed to advocating for policies that support a healthy housing market while equipping REALTORS® with the resources they need to help families achieve homeownership. This legislation is another example of the impact REALTOR® advocacy can have on the future of housing.

As NAR often says, housing affordability begins with housing availability. Creating more opportunities to build and expand housing choices benefits buyers, communities and the local economy alike.
