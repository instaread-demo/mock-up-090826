346 This or That Questions to Ask Your Friends

September 4, 2023September 4, 2023 Viktor Sander B.Sc., B.A.

As adults, it’s easy to get wrapped up in the daily grind and forget the art of genuine connection. But that doesn’t mean the days of sleepovers, philosophical debates, and late-night texts are behind us.

Whether you’re sipping craft beer with your buddies or just texting your BFF after a long workday, these questions are your ticket to not just passing the time, but diving deep. Curious about your friend’s latest crush or partner? Got a hunch that there’s a hilarious or thought-provoking story lurking beneath that rugged exterior? A few “This or That” questions can be the catalysts to unlock those tales.

So, it’s time to turn off autopilot. Whether it’s on a night in, a weekend trip, or just a casual chat, allow yourself to get back to the basics and truly get to know the people in your circle.
