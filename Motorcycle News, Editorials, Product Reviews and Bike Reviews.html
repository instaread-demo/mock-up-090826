Aragon MotoGP Sprint and Full Race Results

Instaread

With the exception of the pole position taken by Marco Bezzecchi (Aprilia), the Aragon round of the MotoGP championship last weekend was the Marc Marquez (Ducati) show.

At one of the counter-clockwise circuits where Marc is almost unbeatable, he won both the Sprint on Saturday and the full GP on Sunday.

On Saturday, Marc was followed home by his brother Alex Marquez (Ducati) in second place, with Bezzecchi finishing third.

On Sunday, Marc had to work his way past Bezzecchi to take the lead, and eventually the win. Pedro Acosta (KTM) finished second and Bezzecchi third.

Jorge Martin (Aprilia) continues to lead the championship, but saw his lead diminish after mediocre results (for him), which included fifth place finishes on both Saturday and Sunday. The championship continues at Misano in two weeks.
