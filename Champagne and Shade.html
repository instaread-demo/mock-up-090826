Could Kenya Moore regain her peach on The Real Housewives of Atlanta?

ByKimberley Spinney|Mar 30, 2025, 8:00 AM EDT

Now that season 16 of the Real Housewives of Atlanta is underway, we have to talk about Kenya Moore. Over the course of her time on this franchise, she has brought so much drama and energy to the series. And it’s hard to think about the show without her.

Even though we have not gotten to the episode that sees Kenya booted from the Real Housewives of Atlanta just yet, we do know that it happened. We also have an idea of what happened. However, we don’t want to talk about that. We want to talk about her future. We want to talk about the potential that she has to possibly get her peach back.

Bad behavior should not be rewarded and certain behavior is basically illegal. So you might be wondering why we are even talking about Kenya Moore coming back to the series. The reason why is that Kenya’s replacement on Season 16 is none other than Phaedra Parks. If you are a long time viewer of RHOA, then you probably remember what went down between her and Kandi Burruss. Not only was it between those two housewives, but it also included Porsha.

The allegations that were levied against Kandi were truly damaging to her reputation, as it was said that she had conspired to potentially assault a fellow housewife (Porsha). At the very least, those rumors could have been damaging. And it all stemmed from rumors that Phaedra spread. Whether or not someone had given her that information, she was a big factor in the allegations and rumors being spread like wildfire.

Is there a precedent for Kenya Moore returning to RHOA in the future?

Knowing that these two women were able to come back and regain their peaches, it actually seems like it leaves the door open for Kenya Moore to return in the future. Does she need a cooling off period from the series? Yes, she does. But knowing that both Phaedra and Porsha were able to make a comeback, has us thinking that this is not the last we’ve seen of Kenya.

However, it’s unlikely that she will be asked back while the person that she attacked is on the series, as Phaedra wasn’t welcomed back to RHOA until Kandi was gone. And obviously she may not have laid hands on anyone, but her actions were certainly an attack.

We know that Bravo is not just going to welcome Kenya back with open arms after what she did. But considering Phaedra was able to reinvent herself, bring in a new audience courtesy of The Traitors, and regain her peach, we think the same could happen for Kenya.

The real question is whether or not Kenya would want to come back. Then again, it’s also whether or not the viewers want her to come back. If the viewers want her back and she is willing to come back, could we see Kenya Moore regaining her peach and joining RHOA in a future season? I think the probability is high.
