Wallet threat level: August 24, 2026

Rock8man, August 24, 2026 | Games
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 5:09
Instaread

There are a few big games out this week, but primary threat has been dubbed ZCOM. This is Star Wars: Zero Company, which has gameplay similar to Firaxis’ XCOM games. The other big games include the early access version of 1666: Amsterdam, the new game from Patrice Desilets, of Prince of Persia: Sands of Time and early Assassin’s Creed fame. Aliens: Fireteam Elite is back with a sequel featuring more co-op Alien shooting. Asobo Studios is also back with another Plague Tale game called Resonance (this one is a prequel, so you don’t need to be familiar with the other Plague Tale games). And finally, the second Metal Gear Solid Master Collection is out, bringing a remastering of Metal Gear Solid 4 to platforms other than the Playstation 3.

Monday (8/24/26):

  • Travellin Cats in USA (PC) – “Find hundreds of cats hidden in multiple iconic cities of the United States of America in this cute hand-drawn hidden object game by Travellin Cats – the original viral hidden cat game creator!”
  • Bit (PC, Xbox One, Xbox Series X/S) – “Bit has the urge to climb. Why does he want to climb? Find out with him across 10 unique worlds and help him find his purpose. You will fight bosses, gain unique abilities and ascend through hand-crafted challenges that test your skill and resolve.”
  • Combolands: Roguelike Citybuilder (PC) – “A mini citybuilding roguelike. Place buildings, stack combos, and master deep strategy in short bursts to create a tiny thriving metropolis!”

Tuesday (8/25/26):

  • Aliens: Fireteam Elite 2 (PC, PS5, Xbox Series X/S now, Switch 2 later this year) – “Aliens: Fireteam Elite 2 drops up to four players into the boots of Colonial Marines deployed to the most hostile corners of the Aliens universe. This is not a rescue mission – it’s a fight for survival.”
  • 1666: Amsterdam (PC early access) – “Every 333 years, something stirs. 1666: Amsterdam is a 3rd person Dark, Story-Led, Action-Adventure where Noa, the Collector, wields witchcraft to uncover demonic entities hiding behind human faces. Investigate by day. Face your demons at night.”
  • Apidya’ Special (PC, PS5, Xbox, Switch) – “1992’s home computer (Amiga) legend Apidya is making a comeback in this authentic 24-bit pixel remake, built from the ground up, but utterly faithful to the original.”
  • Blood Dungeon (PC, PS5, Xbox) – “From the creators of Nidhogg, murder hordes of monsters in this 2D platforming autoshooter with a killer soundtrack. Collect Blood, gold, and bones to increase your powers. Can you survive the Blood Dungeon?”

Wednesday (8/26/26):

  • Brigandine Abyss (PC, PS5, Xbox, Switch 2) – “Play as one of six factions to confront the Abyssloa Empire. Command your troops, develop your bases, and take control of the continent of Meltitea in this tactical strategy RPG! “

Thursday (8/27/26):

  • Metal Gear Solid Master Collection Vol. 2 (PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Switch 2, Switch, PC) – “This second installment to the collection features “Metal Gear Solid 4: Guns of the Patriots” and “Metal Gear Solid: Peace Walker (HD Collection version),” and also a digital soundtrack and “Metal Gear: Ghost Babel” as bonus content. “
  • Aggelos 2 (PS5, PS4, Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One, Switch, PC) – “With the Gods of light about to lose the great battle against the Army of Darkness, the Aggelos must learn to embrace his darker side. Explore two opposing realms full of secrets, puzzles and intense 2D combat in this challenging action-platformer.”
  • AstroScaper (PC) –“Colonize barren asteroids and transform them into thriving space colonies. Discover powerful building synergies, solve satisfying placement puzzles, and journey from one procedurally generated world to the next.”
  • Lou’s Lagoon (PC, PS5, Xbox Series X/S, Switch, Switch 2) – “A cozy exploration-and-crafting adventure: fly your seaplane between tropical islands, collect resources with the Swirler, rebuild storm damaged communities, and unravel the mystery surrounding Uncle Lou’s disappearance.”
  • Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy (PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC) – “Set 15 years before A Plague Tale: Requiem, play as Sophia, a fierce young plunderer, on the run and determined to uncover the secrets of her past. Dive into an original story blending combat, myth and fate with a brand new take in the universe of A Plague Tale.”
  • Star Wars: Zero Company (PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC) – “Command an elite squad through a gritty and authentic story in STAR WARS Zero Company, a single-player turn-based tactics game set in the twilight of the Clone Wars.”
  • The Immortal John Triptych (PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Switch 2, Switch, PC) – “The Immortal John Triptych is the ultimate anthology of the Joe Richardson games. Four Last Things, The Procession to Calvary, and Death of the Reprobate are brought together under one glorious sun…”

Friday (8/28/26):

  • Captain Tsubasa 2: World Fighters (PC, PS5, Xbox Series X/S, Switch) – “The next episode of Tsubasa’s riveting adventure is here! Enjoy the thrill of spectacular soccer actions with 110 playable characters and new sensational super moves. Challenge a friend in the field or support Tsubasa on his Journey, the ball is yours.”
