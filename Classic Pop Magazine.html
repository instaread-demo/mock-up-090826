Pet Shop Boys announce new album ‘A Man From The Future’

Author: Dan Biggane

Read Time:   |  4th September 2026

The eight-track record is a collaboration with Alan Turing biographer Andrew Hodges

Pet Shop Boys have announced A Man From The Future – a new eight-track album created in collaboration with Alan Turing biographer Andrew Hodges exploring the father of theoretical computer science’s pioneering work and legacy.

The work explores eight significant chapters in the life and death of Turing and will be released as an album on 9 October via Parlophone Records.

Alongside music and lyrics by Pet Shop Boys is a narration, spoken by actress Juliet Stevenson, taken from Andrew Hodges’ book Alan Turing: The Enigma, which has been edited and extended by Neil Tennant and Andrew Hodges. The music is a combination of electronics and strings with arrangements by Sven Helbig performed by the Manchester Camerata Octet. Boys from Manchester Grammar School choir also sing on four tracks. The album was produced by Pet Shop Boys and Sven Helbig.

The eight tracks explore the life, death and influence of the pioneering British mathematician and cryptanalyst who laid the foundations of modern computing. Turing’s story unfolds chronologically from his school days, with his initial interest in mathematics and science and his realisation of his sexuality, to his university days at Cambridge, his leading role working for the British Government unlocking the German Enigma machine’s encrypted messages during World War II, his place in the invention of the digital computer, his prediction of Artificial Intelligence, his trial and conviction for a homosexual relationship, and then his suicide in 1954.

Pet Shop Boys say: “Our motive in creating this work was to make a musical memorial to Alan Turing who gave so much to his country and the world and was rewarded for it by being prosecuted for his sexuality. Alan made a massive contribution to the defeat of Germany in World War II and was a pioneer of computing, imagining a world with intelligent computers. He also looked forward to a time when being gay would be accepted as normal. In both his professional and private lives, he was ahead of his time.”

A Man Ahead Of His Time

Neil Tennant and Chris Lowe originally wrote the work with Andrew Hodges in 2012-13 and performed it with the BBC Concert Orchestra and the BBC Singers at a late-night BBC Prom in July 2014 at the Royal Albert Hall. Over a decade later they decided to revise the piece to give it more of an electronic emphasis and Sven Helbig suggested reworking his orchestrations in a more intimate arrangement for eight string players. Andrew Hodges suggested updates to the spoken text.

The BBC will premiere A Man From The Future as a world exclusive this October. BBC Radio 6 Music will broadcast the 2026 edition on Sunday 4 October within Stuart Maconie’s Freak Zone (8-10pm), and Stuart will hear from Pet Shop Boys and Andrew  Hodges about the creative process behind the reworking, and their interest in blending electronic and orchestral music. The piece will then broadcast on BBC Radio 3 later that evening within Unclassified (11.30pm-12.30am) as the Pet Shop Boys and Andrew Hodges join presenter Elizabeth Alker in the studio to reflect on the original performance and its 2026 evolution. Both programmes will be available on BBC Sounds for 30 days after transmission. Audiences outside the UK can listen live on bbc.com/audio.

Pet Shop Boys and Andrew Hodges will also take part in a listening and conversation event for A Man From The Future on Thursday 8 October at the Imperial War Museum North in Manchester. The event will feature a full playback of the album followed by a discussion about the work by Pet Shop Boys and Andrew Hodges. Tickets for the event go on sale on Monday 7 September at 10am BST here. The conversation will also be filmed and made available online shortly afterwards.
