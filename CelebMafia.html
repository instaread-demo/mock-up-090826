Maisie Williams Appears in Who What Wear UK September 2026 Cover Story

Updated September 8, 2026 by Lara Parker ★Add CelebMafia on Google

Maisie Williams moves through four dark romantic looks for Who What Wear UK, from sheer black lace to an enormous white bow and sweeping black skirts.

Maisie Williams has four sharply defined looks in the September 2026 Who What Wear UK feature, and the oversized white bow is the most arresting piece in the set. Maisie Williams in the black bow look wears a fitted black jacket with white cuffs above a short dark skirt, while an enormous white bow sits at the neckline and long pale tails extend well past the knees. Black knee-high boots complete the strongest graphic look of the series.

The other portraits are quieter in shape but richer in surface. One dark dress has narrow shoulder straps, a long skirt and scattered pale floral appliqués, with a short pearl necklace above the neckline. Another look uses a sheer black lace bodice with a full dark skirt and a black head covering. The transparency and dense floral pattern of the lace make this the most delicate outfit in the group.

A fourth image takes the dark palette into a very different scale. Williams stands in a field wearing a long-sleeved black dress with a wide, full skirt. There is little decoration to compete with the volume, so the broad outline itself becomes the fashion statement. Across the Who What Wear UK fashion portraits, black appears in very different forms: close tailoring, sheer lace, floral decoration and large skirt volume.

That range is what gives the feature its strength. The bow look is playful and almost theatrical; the lace outfit is intricate and transparent; the floral dress is softer; the field portrait depends on scale. The wardrobe credits for the feature include Miu Miu, Pauline Dujancourt, Prada and Erdem, while Melanie Rodriguez photographed the September cover story.

The most successful image is still the black tailored look with the oversized white bow. The contrast is immediate, the proportions are unusual, and the long trailing bow sections give the outfit a distinctive silhouette that none of the other looks repeats. I’d rate the series 9/10 for the strength of the bow look and the genuinely varied silhouettes across all four portraits.
