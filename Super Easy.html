What to do if the VPN is not working with Netflix?

If your VPN was previously functioning well but is now experiencing problems, you may try the following fixes:

  • Log out and re-login your Netflix account
  • Clear browser cache and cookies, and refresh Netflix
  • Swap VPN servers, and refresh Netflix
  • Turn off GPS and other location service
  • Use a browser, not the Netflix app
  • Swap the VPN protocols

Note that some VPN products are not able to unblock Netflix, for example, Mullvad, PureVPN, and TunnelBear.

Why didn't I recommend free VPNs?

Free VPNs typically offer a limited number of servers, many of which may already be flagged by Netflix as VPN servers. These free VPN servers are often overloaded, resulting in unstable connections. As a result, attempting to unblock Netflix using a free VPN may prove to be both slow and unsuccessful, even if it does manage to connect. Paid VPNs frequently add new IP addresses to bypass detections.
